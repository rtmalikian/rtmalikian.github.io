META-ANALYSIS · IN PROGRESSCOCHRANE-STYLEBUILT IN PUBLIC

Ambient AI Scribes: Time Saved, Work Shifted, Errors Added

A systematic review and meta-analysis of ambient AI scribes for clinical documentation — effects on documentation time, after-hours work, clinician burnout, note quality and note errors. Health systems are buying these tools at scale on thin evidence; the one prior meta-analysis (Zhao 2025) predates the 2025 randomised trials and pooled note tools in general, not scribes alone. This review pools benefit and error burden together.

Where it stands, honestly: search and screening complete — 1,049 records found (PubMed, Europe PMC, OpenAIRE, ClinicalTrials.gov), 567 after deduplication, dual independent LLM screeners (agreement 0.77, Cohen's κ 0.64) with logged adjudication, 157 studies through to full text; full-text retrieval now underway. No pooled result exists yet — and none will be published until extraction is real.

COMMENTARY · PREPRINT LIVEPUBLIC HEALTH

When a Warning Advertises: The Boomerang Effect in Anti-Drug Campaigns and the Case of Concentrated 7-Hydroxymitragynine

When does an anti-drug campaign become advertising? An evidence-led commentary on reactance and the boomerang effect — from teenage anti-smoking campaigns and DARE to today's concentrated 7-OH products — balanced against the fear-appeal evidence, with a public-health communication strategy that informs without provoking curiosity. 8,470 words, 62 references. Evidence and policy discussion only; no clinical or medication recommendations.
