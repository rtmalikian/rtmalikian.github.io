META-ANALYSIS · SUBMITTED — J CLINICAL EPIDEMIOLOGYCOCHRANE-STYLEBUILT IN PUBLIC

Ambient AI Scribes Meta-Analysis — Documentation Time, Burnout & Note Errors

Systematic review & meta-analysis of ambient AI scribes (AI medical scribes) for clinical documentation: 1,049 records screened, 74 studies included. Pooled documentation time fell 18.9% (low certainty) — and the AI note-error burden is pooled alongside the benefit, so clinician review stays part of the intervention. Submitted to the Journal of Clinical Epidemiology.

DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23565499CC BY 4.0

META-ANALYSIS · JOURNAL SUBMISSION IN PREPARATIONLLM HALLUCINATIONCITATION ACCURACY

LLM Citation Fabrication — How Often AI Models Invent References

Systematic review of large language model citation accuracy in biomedical writing: 63 studies, 148 model arms. Pooled 19.4% of LLM citations were fabricated and only 37.0% fully accurate; retrieval grounding (RAG) cut fabrication to 5.8% vs 31.0% without. The reference-checking evidence base for clinical AI safety.

DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23585717CC BY 4.0
